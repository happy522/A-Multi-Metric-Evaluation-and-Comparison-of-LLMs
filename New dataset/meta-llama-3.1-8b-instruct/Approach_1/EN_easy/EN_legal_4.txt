Commission makes plan to make EU borders safer

Today, the Commission is making a plan to stop people coming into the EU without permission. This plan will make the EU's borders stronger and safer.

The EU has a good system to manage people coming into the EU. But recently, there have been problems at the borders. This summer, many people came to the EU's border in Ceuta. This showed how quickly many people can come to the border and cause problems.

The EU's President, von der Leyen, said that the EU needs to be better prepared for emergencies. This is because emergencies can threaten the EU's safety and unity. They can also cause people to die.

The EU's borders are a shared responsibility. This means that all EU countries need to work together to keep the borders safe. The EU needs to be united and work together to keep the borders safe.

The EU's President has set out five key areas to make the EU's borders safer. These areas are:

1. Working with other countries to stop people coming into the EU without permission. This includes:
- Helping people in their home countries so they don't need to come to the EU.
- Working with other countries to stop people coming into the EU without permission.
- Sharing information with other countries to stop people coming into the EU without permission.

2. Making the EU's borders stronger. This includes:
- Creating a new plan to help the EU respond to emergencies.
- Making Frontex (the EU's border agency) stronger, so it can stop people coming into the EU without permission.
- Giving support to EU countries to make their borders stronger.
- Helping EU countries with emergencies through the EU's civil protection system.
