Ixchiq: Stop giving vaccine to people 65 years old and older

We change the way we use the vaccine Ixchiq. 

- We only use the vaccine when there is a big risk of chikungunya.
- We think carefully about the good and bad things that can happen.

EMA's safety team looked at Ixchiq. They looked at reports of bad side effects.

- We used to stop giving the vaccine to people 65 years old and older. Now we can give it to them again.
- But we only give it to them when there is a big risk of chikungunya.

What the safety team found out

- Many bad side effects happened to people 65 years old and older. They also happened to people with serious health problems.
- These side effects made their health worse. Some people even died.

- The bad side effects are like the symptoms of chikungunya. They include fever, feeling unwell, loss of appetite and confusion. This can cause people to fall.

- Some people get chikungunya-like symptoms. They are usually mild. But some people get very sick.

- There were also cases of encephalitis (inflammation of the brain). This is very rare.

The benefits and risks for older people

- Most bad side effects happened to older people. But Ixchiq can help them make antibodies against chikungunya.
- This can be good for older people who are at risk of getting very sick from chikungunya.

What we decided

- We only give Ixchiq when there is a big risk of chikungunya.
- We think carefully about the good and bad things that can happen.

People with weak immune systems

- We do not give Ixchiq to people with weak immune systems.
- This is because they are at risk of getting very sick from the vaccine.

Information for the public

- Some people got very sick after getting the Ixchiq vaccine. They were mainly 65 years old and older or people with serious health problems.
- The bad side effects are like the symptoms of chikungunya. They include fever, feeling unwell, loss of appetite and confusion. This can cause people to fall.
- There have also been rare cases of encephalitis (inflammation in the brain). If you experience these symptoms, seek medical attention immediately.
- Tell your healthcare professional if your health gets worse soon after vaccination.
- Ixchiq is good at helping people make antibodies against chikungunya. This can be good for older people who are at risk of getting very sick from chikungunya.
- Before you get Ixchiq, your healthcare professional will think about your risk of getting chikungunya and carefully weigh the good and bad things that can happen.
- If you have any questions about the vaccine, talk to your healthcare professional.
