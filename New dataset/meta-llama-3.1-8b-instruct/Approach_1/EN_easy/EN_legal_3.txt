Commission makes European plan for safe AI. 

Today, the European Commission said 'yes' to a big plan from European people. The plan is called 'Creation of a European Digital Space (.IA) for Sovereign AI Governance'. 

The people who made the plan want the Commission to make a safe place for computers and phones to talk to each other. They want the Commission to talk to other groups to make sure this safe place is protected. 

The plan has three main goals: 

1. Make a safe place for computers and phones to talk to each other in the European Union. 
2. Protect the special name for this safe place. 
3. Make sure people's personal information is safe when they use this safe place. 

The Commission said the plan is legal. But they have not looked at the plan's ideas yet. They will only make a decision if one million European people agree with the plan. 

Next steps
The people who made the plan have six months to ask one million European people to agree with the plan. If they get one million signatures, the Commission will make a decision. 

Background
This plan is a special tool for European people to tell the Commission what they want. It was made a long time ago. The Commission has said 'yes' to 136 plans like this before. 

The plan only says what the people who made it think. It does not say what the Commission thinks.
