Benefits of sodium oxybate still good for people with alcohol problems
More studies needed to know how well it works
The European medicines committee has said that the good things about sodium oxybate syrup and liquid (175 mg/ml) are still more important than the bad things when used to help people with alcohol problems. 

This medicine can help people who have a bad reaction to alcohol and want to stop drinking for good. However, we need more studies to know how well it works for this use. 

The committee looked at the evidence and found that the studies done after the medicine was approved had some problems. This means we are not sure how well it works. The committee also said that there have been improvements in how we do studies since the first studies were done. 

So, the committee has asked the companies to do new studies to learn more about how well the medicine works. If they do these studies, the medicine can stay on the market. 

The committee did not find any new safety concerns. The medicine is still safe to use. 

The committee's opinion will now go to the European Commission, which will make a final decision. 

Information for patients
•	In Austria and Italy, this medicine is used to help people with alcohol problems. 
•	The medicine will still be available in Italy and Austria, but new studies will be done to learn more about how well it works. 
•	There are no new safety concerns about the medicine. 
•	If you have questions about your treatment, talk to your doctor or pharmacist.
