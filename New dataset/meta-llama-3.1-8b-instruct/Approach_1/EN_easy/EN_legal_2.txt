The European Commission has fined Kingspan €40 million. 

Kingspan did something wrong. They gave the Commission (a group that makes rules) bad information. This was during a time when Kingspan wanted to buy another company called Trimo. 

Kingspan and Trimo both make things for buildings. They make special panels for buildings. 

The Commission looked into this and found that Kingspan gave bad information. They did this in four ways:

1. They said something that was not true about how well their product was selling.
2. They did not give the Commission the right information about how they were doing in the market.
3. They said some of their leaders were not involved in important decisions, but they were.
4. They did not give the Commission the right information about how they were making their product better.

The Commission can fine companies up to 1% of their money if they give bad information. They looked at how bad Kingspan's actions were and decided to fine them €10 million for each of the four times they gave bad information. This makes a total of €40 million.

The Commission thinks this fine is fair and will help prevent companies from giving bad information in the future.
