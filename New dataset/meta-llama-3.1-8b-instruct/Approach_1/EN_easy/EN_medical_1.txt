EMA says stop selling Tavneos medicine
Benefits not good enough compared to risks
EMA's group of experts looked at the medicine Tavneos (avacopan). They decided that the medicine's benefits are not good enough to outweigh its risks. 

Tavneos is used to treat two rare diseases that affect the blood vessels. These diseases are called granulomatosis with polyangiitis (GPA) and microscopic polyangiitis (MPA). 

The experts looked at a study that included 331 patients with these diseases. The study compared Tavneos to a dummy treatment, and both were used with other medicines. 

However, the experts found that the study was not done correctly. The data from the study was wrong and could not be trusted. The experts also looked at other data, but it was not enough to prove that Tavneos is good for patients. 

The group of experts decided that the medicine's marketing authorisation should be stopped in the European Union. This means that Tavneos will not be available for new patients. Patients who are already taking Tavneos should be switched to other medicines. 

Tavneos can cause serious liver problems. Patients taking this medicine should have their liver function checked regularly. If liver problems are suspected, the medicine must be stopped immediately.
