Benefits of sodium oxybate are still greater than risks for people dependent on alcohol
More studies are needed to show how well it works

The EMA medicine committee says the benefits of sodium oxybate liquid medicine are greater than the risks. It is used to treat sudden alcohol withdrawal and to help people stay away from alcohol. However, the current data has some limits. More studies are needed to show exactly how well the medicine works for these uses.

France asked for this review. They were worried about how well the medicine works based on three studies. They also worried about the risk of people misusing the medicine because it affects the brain. They wanted to check if the safety rules are good enough.

The committee looked at all the evidence. They found that past studies had problems with how they were done. This makes us unsure about the results. Science has improved since those studies were done. Now, the committee says the medicine companies must do new studies. If the European Commission agrees, the companies must finish these studies to keep permission to sell the medicine.

Regarding safety, nothing new was found. The risks are what we already knew. There are no new worries about people misusing the medicine. The current safety rules do not need to change.

The committee decided the medicine is still good to use. This is true as long as the companies do the new studies to prove it works.

This advice will go to the European Commission. They will make the final decision for all EU countries.

Information for patients
•	In Austria and Italy, this medicine helps adults stop drinking alcohol. In Austria, it also helps people stay stopped.
•	The medicine will still be available in these countries. New studies will happen to learn more about how well it works.
•	There are no new safety worries.
•	If you take this medicine and have questions, talk to your doctor or pharmacist.
