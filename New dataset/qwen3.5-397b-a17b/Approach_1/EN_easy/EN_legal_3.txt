European Commission Accepts Citizen Proposal for European AI Domain

Today, the European Commission officially started a citizen proposal. It is called "Creation of a European Digital Space (.IA) for Sovereign AI Governance".

The people behind this proposal want the Commission to make rules for safe digital spaces. They also want the Commission to talk to international groups (ISO and ICANN) to protect the ".IA" internet domain. They have three main goals:
1. Make EU rules for testing ethical AI.
2. Protect the ".IA" domain as a European asset.
3. Make sure the ".IA" domain uses secure digital IDs, checks AI algorithms, and protects personal data.

This proposal meets the basic rules, so the Commission says it is allowed by law. The Commission has not yet looked at the details. Accepting the proposal now does not mean the Commission agrees with it. The Commission will only make a decision if the proposal gets enough support.

Next Steps
The organizers have six months to start collecting signatures. They have 12 months to collect them. They need at least one million valid signatures from people in at least seven EU countries. If they get enough, the Commission must respond and explain what it will do.

Background
The European Citizens' Initiative started in 2012. It lets one million citizens from at least seven EU countries ask the Commission to make new laws. To be accepted, the proposal must be something the Commission can do. It must not be rude, silly, or annoying. It must not go against EU values or basic human rights.

Since the start, the Commission has accepted 136 proposals.

The ideas in this proposal belong to the organizers. They do not represent the views of the Commission.
