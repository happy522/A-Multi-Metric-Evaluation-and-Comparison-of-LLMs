Ixchiq Vaccine: Rules for People 65 and Older Are Changing

The vaccine should only be used if there is a high risk of chikungunya. Doctors must check if it is safe for you.

Safety experts checked the Ixchiq vaccine. This vaccine helps prevent chikungunya disease. They looked at reports of serious health problems after people got the shot.

Before, people 65 and older could not get the vaccine while experts were checking it. Now, this rule is removed.

But there is a new rule for everyone. The vaccine should only be given if there is a high chance of getting chikungunya. Doctors must think carefully about the good and bad effects for each person.

What We Know About Safety

Serious problems happened mostly in people 65 and older. They also happened in people with other health problems. These problems made their health worse. Some people had to go to the hospital. Some people died.

Many side effects look like chikungunya disease. Symptoms include fever, feeling sick, not wanting to eat, and confusion. Confusion can cause falls. Most symptoms are mild. But about 2 out of 100 adults may get severe symptoms.

Experts also looked at brain swelling (encephalitis). Symptoms include confusion, sleepiness, fever, and headache. This is rare. We do not know exactly how often it happens.

Benefits and Risks for Older People

Most serious problems happened in older people. But the vaccine works well. It helps the body fight the chikungunya virus. This is very helpful for older people because they can get very sick from chikungunya.

So, experts say the vaccine should only be used if there is a high risk of infection. Doctors must weigh the benefits and risks carefully.

People with Weak Immune Systems

Doctors must not give this vaccine to people with weak immune systems. This includes people with certain diseases or medical treatments. These people can get very sick from vaccines made with a weak form of the virus, like Ixchiq.

This rule was already in place. It stays the same. The vaccine information will be updated with these new rules.

The vaccine was approved in Europe in June 2024. About 36,000 doses were used around the world before this review started.

Information for You

• Some people had serious side effects. This happened mostly to people 65 and older or people with long-term health problems.
• Most side effects look like chikungunya disease. They include fever, feeling unwell, not wanting to eat, and confusion. Confusion can cause falls.
• Rarely, people got brain swelling. Symptoms include confusion, sleepiness, fever, and headache. Get medical help right away if you have these symptoms.
• Tell your doctor if your health gets worse soon after the shot.
• The vaccine works well to protect against chikungunya. This is helpful for older people who might get very sick.
• Before you get the vaccine, your doctor will check your risk of chikungunya. They will decide if the benefits are worth the risks for you.
• Ask your doctor if you have questions.
