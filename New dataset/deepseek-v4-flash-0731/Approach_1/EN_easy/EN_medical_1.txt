EMA says: Stop using the medicine Tavneos.

EMA’s medicine experts looked at Tavneos very carefully. They found that the good things about Tavneos are no longer sure to be more than the bad things. So EMA says Tavneos should not be sold in Europe anymore.

Tavneos is a medicine for adults with two rare sicknesses. These sicknesses hurt the blood tubes in the body. The sicknesses are called GPA and MPA.

EMA started this check because new information came up. This new information made EMA question if the main study about Tavneos was done properly.

In the study, 331 people with GPA or MPA took part. Some people took Tavneos for 52 weeks. Other people took a fake medicine called placebo. Both groups also took steroid medicine for 20 weeks. Steroids help reduce swelling in the body. Everyone also got standard treatment.

At first, EMA thought Tavneos worked as well as the steroid medicine. It seemed to help people get better and stay better for longer.

But after looking at all the information again, EMA found problems. The study was not done the right way. The information from the study was wrong and could mislead people. So EMA cannot trust that study to show Tavneos works.

Other information from after the study is not enough to prove Tavneos helps people.

So EMA says Tavneos should be stopped in Europe. EMA also talked to patients and doctors about this. If the European Commission agrees, Tavneos will not be allowed in Europe.

EMA says no new people should start taking Tavneos. People who are already taking Tavneos should switch to other medicines that work the same way.

Tavneos can hurt the liver. It can cause liver damage. It can also cause a rare problem where the small tubes in the liver get damaged and disappear. This can be very bad and even cause death.

So for people taking Tavneos, doctors must check their liver often. If someone has taken Tavneos for less than 3 months, check the liver every 2 weeks until 3 months are done. If someone has taken Tavneos for more than 3 months, check the liver every 4 weeks for up to 6 months. Then check as needed. If doctors think the liver problem is happening, stop Tavneos right away.
