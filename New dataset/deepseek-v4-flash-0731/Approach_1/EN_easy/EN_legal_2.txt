The European Commission has fined Kingspan €40 million. This is because Kingspan gave wrong or confusing information during a check about buying another company called Trimo. The check happened in 2021. Kingspan did not buy Trimo in the end.

Kingspan and Trimo make special panels for buildings. These panels help keep buildings warm and are used in many industrial and commercial buildings.

What Kingspan did wrong

In November 2022, the Commission started looking into whether Kingspan gave wrong or confusing information on purpose or by mistake. This was during the check about buying Trimo.

On 19 March 2024, the Commission told Kingspan about six problems. These problems were about important information for the check. After Kingspan answered, the Commission looked again and removed two of the six problems. Now, the Commission says Kingspan did four wrong things. Kingspan gave:

- Wrong or confusing information about how it tracks how much its panels are used compared to others.
- Wrong or confusing information about records of bids it won or lost. This information helps show how companies compete.
- Wrong information about what its board members did. This includes their work on buying Trimo, other planned buys, and business plans.
- Wrong or confusing information about its research and development for the panels. This information is important to understand Kingspan's place in the market.

Kingspan gave this wrong information in forms or answers during the check.

The fine

The Commission can fine a company up to 1% of its total money if it gives wrong or confusing information on purpose or by mistake. The fine depends on how serious the problem is, how long it lasted, and other things. The fine must also stop others from doing the same.

The Commission says these four problems are serious. They made the check harder and stopped the Commission from doing its job well. Kingspan should have known its duties under the rules. So, the problems were at least by mistake. Kingspan said some important papers did not exist, gave wrong facts, or said it could not give information that only it had.

Because of this, the Commission fined Kingspan €10 million for each problem. That is €40 million in total. The Commission thinks this fine is fair and will stop others from doing the same.
