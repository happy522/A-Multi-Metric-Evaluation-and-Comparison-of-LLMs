EMA says medicines with levamisole should be removed from the EU market.

Levamisole is used to treat worm infections in adults and children. However, a safety review found that the medicine is too dangerous.

The review found a serious side effect called leukoencephalopathy. This condition damages the white matter in the brain. This can make a person very sick or even cause death. It is hard for doctors to find and treat.

This brain damage can happen after just one dose. Symptoms might not start until several months after the treatment. There is no way to know who is at risk or how to stop it from happening.

Because there are other safe medicines to treat worm infections, the EMA decided that levamisole is no longer safe to use.

**Information for patients**

*   EMA recommends that medicines with levamisole be removed from the EU.
*   Levamisole can cause serious damage to the brain.
*   There are other medicines you can use for worm infections.
*   If you have used levamisole, see a doctor immediately if you have:
    *   Weak muscles
    *   Trouble speaking
    *   Confusion
    *   Trouble moving your body
*   These problems can happen even after one dose or several months later.
*   If you have questions about your treatment or your child's treatment, talk to your doctor.
