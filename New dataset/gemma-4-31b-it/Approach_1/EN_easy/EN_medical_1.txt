EMA says the medicine Tavneos should be stopped.

The benefits of the medicine are no longer seen as better than the risks. Tavneos is used for adults with two rare blood vessel diseases called GPA and MPA.

EMA looked at the main study used to approve the medicine. They found that the study was not done correctly. The information in the study was wrong and misleading. Because of this, EMA cannot prove that the medicine actually works.

EMA recommends that Tavneos should no longer be sold in the European Union.

**What this means for patients:**
* No new patients should start taking Tavneos.
* Patients already taking Tavneos should switch to a different medicine.

**Warning about the liver:**
Tavneos can cause serious liver damage. Some people have died from this. Doctors must check the liver function of patients until they stop the medicine completely.

* If a patient took Tavneos for less than three months: The liver must be checked every two weeks until three months have passed since they started the medicine.
* If a patient took Tavneos for more than three months: The liver must be checked every four weeks for up to six months.

If a doctor thinks the liver is damaged, the patient must stop taking Tavneos immediately.
