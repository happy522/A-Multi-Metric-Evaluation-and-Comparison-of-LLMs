The European Commission fined the company Kingspan €40 million. This is because Kingspan gave wrong or misleading information during a 2021 investigation. 

The investigation was about Kingspan's plan to buy another company called Trimo. Both companies make special panels used to build and insulate industrial buildings. In the end, Kingspan decided not to buy Trimo.

**What happened?**
The Commission found that Kingspan gave wrong information in four areas:
1. How they track how much of their product is used compared to others.
2. Information about "bidding data" (records of jobs they won or lost).
3. What the company's board members knew about buying other companies and business plans.
4. Information about their research and development (R&D).

This information is very important for the Commission to decide if a merger is fair for the market.

**The fines**
The law says the Commission can fine companies if they give wrong information. 

The Commission decided the fine should be €40 million because:
* The mistakes were serious.
* They made the investigation difficult.
* Kingspan should have known the rules.
* Kingspan claimed some important documents did not exist when they actually did.

Kingspan must pay €10 million for each of the four mistakes.
