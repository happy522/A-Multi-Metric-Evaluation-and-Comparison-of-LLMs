EU consumer groups are taking action to protect people who play video games. They are working with nine game companies to make sure players are treated fairly.

The groups want game companies to follow these rules:
* **Clear prices:** Show the real money cost of items in the game.
* **No hidden costs:** Do not use confusing currency exchanges to hide the real price.
* **No forced buying:** Players should not have to buy too much virtual money to enjoy the game.
* **Clear information:** Give easy-to-understand information before a player buys something.
* **Right to change mind:** Tell players they can get their money back within 14 days for things they didn't use.
* **Fair rules:** The game's terms and conditions must be fair and easy to read.
* **Protect children:** Make sure there are safety rules for children and other vulnerable people.

**Background**
The CPC Network is a group of authorities that make sure EU consumer laws are followed. 

In 2025, they talked to the game industry, but the companies did not make enough changes. The group then checked several games, including:
* Hunt: Showdown 1896
* Forge of Empires
* Candy Crush Saga
* Minecraft
* Mech Arena
* Gardenscapes
* Valorant
* Clash of Clans
* For Honor

The group is also checking Activision Blizzard (for the games Diablo Immortal and Call of Duty Mobile). They are looking at how money is spent, how personal data is used, if the games are too addictive, and if children are being targeted with ads.
