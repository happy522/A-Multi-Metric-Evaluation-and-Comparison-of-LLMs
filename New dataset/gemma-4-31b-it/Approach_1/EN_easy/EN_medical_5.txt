**Sodium oxybate is still useful for people with alcohol problems, but more research is needed**

A group of experts (the CHMP) looked at medicines called sodium oxybate syrup and oral solution. These medicines help people who are stopping drinking alcohol (withdrawal) and help them stay sober.

The experts decided that these medicines are still helpful and the benefits are greater than the risks. However, they need more proof that the medicines work well.

**Why was this review done?**
The French medicines agency asked for a review. They were worried that the medicines might not work as well as thought. They were also worried that people might misuse the medicine because it affects the brain.

**What did the review find?**
The experts looked at old and new studies. They found that some of the studies were not done perfectly. Because science has improved, the experts want the companies to do new, better studies to prove the medicines work. If the companies do not do these studies, they might not be allowed to sell the medicines anymore.

**Is the medicine safe?**
Yes. The review found no new safety problems. There is no new evidence that people are misusing the medicine more than before. The safety rules stay the same.

**What happens next?**
The European Commission will make the final legal decision for all EU countries.

**Information for patients**
* In Austria and Italy, sodium oxybate is used to help adults stop drinking alcohol and stay sober.
* These medicines will still be available in Italy and Austria.
* There are no new safety worries.
* If you take this medicine and have questions, please talk to your doctor or pharmacist.
