The European Commission has registered a new European Citizens' Initiative (ECI). This is a request from citizens to create a special European digital space for AI (Artificial Intelligence).

**What do the organisers want?**
The organisers want the EU to:
1. Create safe and ethical places to test AI.
2. Protect a special internet address ending in ".IA" so Europe can control its own AI technology.
3. Make sure this system protects personal data and uses secure digital IDs.

**What happens now?**
The Commission says the request follows the legal rules. However, the Commission has not decided if they agree with the ideas yet.

To move forward, the organisers must:
* Start collecting signatures within six months.
* Collect at least one million signatures from EU citizens.
* Get signatures from at least seven different EU countries.

If they get enough signatures, the Commission must look at the proposal and decide if they will take action.

**What is a European Citizens' Initiative?**
An ECI is a tool that lets EU citizens suggest new laws. If one million people from seven countries sign a petition, the European Commission must respond.

The views in this initiative belong to the organisers, not the European Commission.
