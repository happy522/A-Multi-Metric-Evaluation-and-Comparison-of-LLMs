The European Commission is taking Bulgaria to the European Court of Justice. This is because Bulgaria did not follow EU rules for the electricity market.

The EU has a law (Directive 2019/944) to make electricity markets fair, open, and cheap for everyone. It also protects people who buy electricity.

Bulgaria had to put these rules into its own national laws by December 31, 2020. The Commission warned Bulgaria several times, but the country still has not fixed all the problems.

Some of the missing rules are about:
* How other companies can use the electricity network.
* How to get permission for new power plants.
* The right for customers to switch electricity providers together.
* How the electricity network handles changes in power use.
* How data is managed and how the electricity regulator works.

The Commission wants the Court to make Bulgaria pay a fine.

The Commission does this to make sure all EU countries follow the same laws. This helps protect consumers and ensures clean, affordable energy for everyone. Bulgaria is one of 10 EU countries that have not yet followed these specific electricity rules.
