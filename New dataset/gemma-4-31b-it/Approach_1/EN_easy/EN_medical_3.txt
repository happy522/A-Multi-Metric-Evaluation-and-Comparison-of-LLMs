**Ixchiq: New rules for the chikungunya vaccine**

**What is happening?**
There is a vaccine called Ixchiq that protects people from a virus called chikungunya. 

For a short time, people aged 65 and older were told not to take it. A safety group (PRAC) looked at the vaccine to see if it was safe. Now, people aged 65 and older can take the vaccine again.

**When should people take the vaccine?**
The safety group says the vaccine should only be used if:
1. There is a high risk of catching chikungunya.
2. A doctor decides that the vaccine helps more than it harms.

**What are the risks?**
Some people had serious side effects. This happened mostly to people aged 65 and older or people who were already sick. 

Some side effects feel like the virus itself. These include:
* Fever
* Feeling unwell
* Not wanting to eat
* Confusion (which can make people fall)

In very rare cases, the vaccine caused inflammation of the brain (encephalitis). This can cause sleepiness, fever, and headaches.

**Why still use the vaccine for older people?**
Older people are more likely to get very sick from the chikungunya virus. The vaccine works well to protect them. This is why doctors can still give it to them, but they must be careful.

**Who must NOT take the vaccine?**
People with a weak immune system must not take Ixchiq. This is because the vaccine uses a live (but weakened) virus, which can be dangerous for them.

**Information for you:**
* Some people, especially those 65+ or those with other health problems, had serious side effects.
* If you feel confused, very sleepy, or have a bad headache after the vaccine, see a doctor right away.
* Tell your doctor if your health gets worse after the vaccine.
* Your doctor will talk to you about the risks and benefits before giving you the vaccine.
* If you have questions, ask your doctor.
