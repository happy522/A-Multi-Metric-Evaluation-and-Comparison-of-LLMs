The EU Commission has a new 5-point plan. This plan helps the EU stop and handle large groups of people arriving illegally at the borders.

The EU already has rules to manage migration. These rules are working, and fewer people are crossing borders illegally than in 2021. However, recent events show that the EU needs better tools for emergencies. When too many people arrive at once, it can be dangerous and people can die. Protecting the borders is a job for all EU countries together.

President von der Leyen suggested five main areas for action. Here are the first two:

**1. Working with other countries to stop illegal migration**
The EU wants to work closer with countries outside the EU. They will:
* Help people find jobs and skills in their own countries so they do not feel they must leave.
* Create better warning systems to know when a crisis is coming.
* Share more information between EU police and border agencies and partner countries.
* Use better technology to watch borders and find risks early.

**2. Making EU borders stronger**
Each EU country is responsible for its own borders, but the EU will help more. They will:
* Create a new "Emergency Response Framework" to keep borders safe during crises.
* Give Frontex (the border agency) more power, more staff, and better equipment.
* Give extra help to countries with the most difficult border areas.
* Send emergency help to countries that ask for it.
